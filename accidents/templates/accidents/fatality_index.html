How the groups are made
Casualty
An injured driver or rider, a passenger or a pedestrian. Killed = injury 1 (Fatal); injured = 2 (Hospitalised) or 3 (Injured not hospitalised).
Foreign group
At least one vehicle in the crash is registered abroad: "30 Registered in" is Foreign, or the registration code is 12 (Foreign).
Ghanaian group
Every vehicle in the crash is registered in Ghana: "Registered in" is Ghana, or the registration code is a region or the army (1 to 11).
Left out
No foreign vehicle, but at least one vehicle whose registration is not known (also temporary or unregistered plates).
Fatality Index
Killed per 100 casualties.
